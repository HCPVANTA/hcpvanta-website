How it works

Every campaign follows the same four steps, and nothing goes out without your approval.

  1. Agree your ideal buyer

    A short onboarding call to understand your product, your best customers and the roles you want to meet.

  2. Build your target list

    We research named buyers at relevant trusts, hospitals and care groups who match your brief.

  3. Run personal outreach

    Tailored emails, LinkedIn messages and calls, written in your voice and approved by you first.

  4. Book and brief

    Meetings go straight into your calendar with a note on who you're meeting and why they're interested.

You pay for meetings, not promises.

No retainer to start. You pay a fixed fee for each qualified meeting that takes place.

Before we begin, we agree in writing what counts as a qualified meeting: the right role, at a target organisation, with a real interest in what you offer. No-shows aren't charged.

You get a simple weekly report showing who we've contacted, who replied and which meetings are booked.

We keep prospect data for no longer than 24 months after our last contact with you, then delete it. If you ask us not to contact you, we keep a minimal record so we can make sure you aren't contacted again.
