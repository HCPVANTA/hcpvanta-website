Questions people ask.

Who owns the website, CRM and data?

You do. We build everything in accounts registered in your name, and hand over full access at the end of the project.

Do you handle patient data?

No. We don’t build systems that store patient health records, and we don’t market to patients on your behalf. If you need patient-facing tools such as booking, we can point you to established healthcare platforms that meet the required standards and link them from your website.

How long does setup take?

It depends on the package and how quickly your content is ready. We agree a timescale with you before any work starts.

What will it cost to run?

You pay running costs such as your domain, email and hosting directly to the providers. We’ll explain exactly what they are before you commit.

Do I have to take both services?

No. Many clients only need the setup. Suppliers can add meetings with healthcare buyers at any point.

How do you contact buyers for suppliers?

With personal emails, LinkedIn messages and calls to business contacts only. Every message includes a simple way to opt out, and we follow UK data protection and electronic marketing rules.
